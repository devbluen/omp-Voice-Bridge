Voice Bridge - client de voz para SA-MP
SA-MP 0.3.7 R1, R2, R3, R4, R5 e 0.3.DL
=========================================

INSTALACAO
  1. Copie o voice-bridge.asi para a pasta do GTA San Andreas
     (a mesma pasta do gta_sa.exe).
  2. Se tiver o sampvoice.asi, apague. O Voice Bridge funciona nos
     servidores SampVoice tambem.

  E preciso um ASI Loader (a maioria das instalacoes de SA-MP/CLEO ja tem).
  Se o mod nao carregar, instale o "Silent's ASI Loader".

NO JOGO
  F11   abre o menu de voz:
          Status      conectado ou nao, ping e teclas de falar
          Som         volume, som 3D, eco do ambiente, bipe da tecla
          Microfone   dispositivo, ganho, filtro de ruido e teste
          Jogadores   volume e silenciar cada jogador
          Interface   tamanho e posicao do icone (arraste com o menu aberto)
  As teclas para falar sao definidas pelo servidor.

PROBLEMAS
  O mod nao carrega   falta o ASI Loader ou o sampvoice.asi ainda esta la
  Nao escuto ninguem  confira o volume em F11 > Som
  Detalhes            voicebridge.log, na pasta do GTA
  Configuracoes       voicebridge.ini (apague para voltar ao padrao)

Voice Bridge - voice chat client for SA-MP
SA-MP 0.3.7 R1, R2, R3, R4, R5 and 0.3.DL

INSTALLATION
  1. Copy voice-bridge.asi into the GTA San Andreas folder
     (next to gta_sa.exe).
  2. Delete sampvoice.asi if you have it. Voice Bridge also works on
     SampVoice servers.

  An ASI loader is required (most SA-MP/CLEO installs have one).
  If the mod does not load, install "Silent's ASI Loader".

IN GAME
  F11   opens the voice menu:
          Status       connected or not, ping and push-to-talk keys
          Sound        volume, 3D sound, room echo, push-to-talk beep
          Microphone   device, gain, noise gate and test
          Players      volume and mute for each player
          Interface    size and icon position (drag it while the menu is open)
  Push-to-talk keys are chosen by the server.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  Mod does not load    missing ASI loader, or sampvoice.asi is still there
  Can't hear anyone    check the volume in F11 > Sound
  Details              voicebridge.log in the GTA folder
  Settings             voicebridge.ini (delete it to reset)
